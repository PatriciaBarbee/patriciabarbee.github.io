🌻 Patricia, the Woman

Call me Patsy.

Where I come from

Chicago made me. I say it plain: I'm a fly girl from the city of Chicago, and everything I build carries that city's music, its history, and its refusal to shrink. Des Moines has been home for five years now — the second time this city has held me — and this time I came with a purpose bigger than a paycheck.

I've spent more than twenty years in early childhood education. I know little girls. I know what they carry into a room before anyone teaches them anything — and I know what the world hands them too early.

How I got here

In the spring of 2022 I started substitute teaching in Des Moines Public Schools. Room after room, I kept meeting the same truth: brilliant girls, short on access. Not short on talent — never that. Short on somebody whose whole job was to stand in the gap for them.

So I built the thing I kept looking for. Project Bridging started as an idea I couldn't put down — it still wakes me up at night, if I'm honest — and it has grown into a sisterhood of girls in grades 5 through 12 who are learning to lead, to serve, and to believe their own evidence.

What I believe

Talent is everywhere, but access and opportunity are not.

I create under the name Patsy on Purpose — because none of this is accidental. My series The Art of Beecoming is where I work out loud: who I am outside of what I do for other people, what I know about myself now, and how a woman keeps becoming. I journal on paper. I love Black culture and Chicago history, a good planner, R&B, and a museum day. And I believe a girl who has seen what's possible can never be talked out of it.
