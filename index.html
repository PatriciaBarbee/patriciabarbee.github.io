About Project Bridging

Bridging gaps. Expanding opportunities.

Project Bridging is the signature youth development initiative of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs (BARYP). Our work connects culturally affirming mentorship, leadership development, education, wellness, advocacy, meaningful community engagement, and experiential learning.

Why we exist

Every young woman deserves access to opportunities, resources, relationships, and experiences that support her growth. Yet many encounter barriers to enrichment, positive mentorship, career exploration, wellness resources, and supportive networks during critical stages of life.

Project Bridging exists to help bridge those gaps. We create spaces where young women are supported, challenged, encouraged, and equipped to navigate challenges and opportunities while strengthening confidence, knowledge, relationships, and skills.

We believe in opportunity and access. We recognize young women for far more than the challenges they may face.

Who we serve

We serve young women throughout Des Moines and Central Iowa, with our primary pathway focused on grades 5–12 and Junior Project Bridging introducing kindergarten through fourth grade participants to age-appropriate development and enrichment.

The framework behind the work

Seven conditions. One whole young woman.

A young woman’s growth does not happen in a vacuum. The Seven Vital Conditions for Health and Well-Being are the conditions every community needs for its people to thrive — and they are the conditions Project Bridging’s work touches every day, with racial equity, gender equity, and justice running through all seven.

Belonging & Civic Muscle

Sisterhood, mentorship, youth voice, and a seat at the table — every girl seen, heard, and connected.

Thriving Natural World

Healthy neighborhoods and safe spaces to gather, play, and grow are part of a whole, healthy life.

Basic Needs for Health & Safety

Wellness, menstrual equity, hygiene essentials, and safe, supportive spaces where girls can simply be.

Humane Housing

A stable home underpins everything else. We stand with families facing housing barriers and connect them to community resources.

Meaningful Work & Wealth

Financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and college and career readiness — building futures that pay forward.

Lifelong Learning

Bee University, literacy, STEM, and educational experiences that reach far beyond the classroom.

Reliable Transportation

Getting to school, programs, and opportunities is access itself. Transportation barriers are opportunity barriers.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about the Hive

What grades does Project Bridging serve?

Our primary pathway serves grades 5–12. Junior Project Bridging is designed for kindergarten through fourth grade.

What do mentees do in the program?

Activities can include mentorship, educational enrichment, wellness, practical skills, leadership development, civic engagement, reflection, community service, and educational experiences.

How can I enroll my young woman?

Use the enrollment contact link above to ask about the current intake process and availability.

How can I support Project Bridging?

Ask about volunteering, mentorship, sponsorship, in-kind contributions, financial giving, educational access, or partnering with the Hive.

Does Project Bridging offer educational trips?

Educational excursions are part of its experiential learning approach. Specific dates, destinations, and participation requirements are shared when plans are confirmed.
