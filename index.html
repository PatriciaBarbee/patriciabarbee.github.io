About Project Bridging

Who We Are

Established in 2022, Project Bridging is the signature youth development initiative of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs (BARYP), serving young women in grades 5–12 throughout Des Moines and surrounding communities.

Project Bridging is more than a mentorship program. It is a community of young women, mentors, families, and partners brought together through shared experiences, meaningful relationships, and opportunities for personal growth.

150+

Young women reached through Project Bridging programming, experiences, and events — and growing.

The Framework Behind the Work

Developing the Whole Young Woman

Our approach recognizes that young women need more than mentorship alone. Through seven interconnected areas of support and opportunity, Project Bridging addresses barriers to participation, strengthens relationships, expands access to resources, and creates meaningful experiences during critical stages of growth and development.

Guided by our theory of change, we connect access, belonging, empowerment, leadership, and community impact to support young women throughout their journey of Beecoming.
