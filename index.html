The one-sentence version

Project Bridging empowers girls through culturally affirming mentorship, leadership development, social justice education, and transformative experiences that expand access, address inequities, and create opportunities for personal, academic, and professional growth.

Talent is everywhere. Access and opportunity are not. That's the gap — and closing it is the work.
