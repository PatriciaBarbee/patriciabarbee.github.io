The organization

Bridging Youth Advancement and Resource Programs (BARYP) is a nonprofit organization recognized through the State of Iowa, headquartered in Des Moines. Its federal 501(c)(3) status was filed as private and is being changed to public; that updated federal paper is pending. Its signature program is Project Bridging. One board governs the house; each program is a room in it. The motto over the door: From Risk to Resource.

BARYP exists to enfranchise the disenfranchised — to stand where access runs out and build the bridge across it: mentorship, leadership development, STEAM learning through Bee University, and transformative experiences that put girls in rooms they were never expected to enter.

How I lead

I run this organization on a simple discipline: one message, set weekly; content created, not chased; asks made plainly and with dignity; community tended like family; systems reviewed every Friday so no good idea ever has to live in my head alone. Our partners are exactly that — partners. Nobody here is rescued. Girls earn, villages invest, and everybody's dignity stays intact.

On this page, over time, you'll find the ED's desk notes: what we're building, what it costs, what we've learned, and what we're asking of the village next.
