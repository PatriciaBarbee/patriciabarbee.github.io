🐝 Project Bridging

Equity Through Empowerment.

Our mission

Project Bridging empowers girls through culturally affirming mentorship, leadership development, social justice education, and transformative experiences that expand access, address inequities, and create opportunities for personal, academic, and professional growth.

Empowering Girls. Expanding Access. Advancing Social Justice. Creating Opportunities. Developing Leaders.

Project Bridging is not about fixing girls. It is about addressing the gaps in access, opportunity, representation, and equity around them.

Who we serve

Girls in grades 5 through 12 in Central Iowa — 25+ girls strong today, on field trips, in workshops, and in sisterhood. They lead their own house through the Mentee Leadership Government: Captains, a Money Team, an Ask Team, an Events Crew, a Story Team, and a Gratitude Team.

How it works

Three pillars hold everything: Character · Leadership · Service. The girls walk The Bee Path™ — Discover. Lead. Serve. — three practices, woven together, never a ladder.

Inside the house: Bee University, our STEAM learning academy; Beecoming Her Best Self (identity and confidence); Project Pocketbook (financial literacy and life skills); Bee the Boss (youth entrepreneurship); Thynk Pynk (wellness), and a monthly rhythm of books, field trips, and service.

Four commitments guide every decision: 👑 Empowerment · 🌉 Access · 🌍 Opportunity · ⚖️ Social Justice.

The pathway

Access → Belonging → Empowerment → Leadership → Legacy
