A few words about us

Built for young women. Rooted in community. Focused on their future.

Project Bridging is the signature youth development initiative of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs. We create culturally affirming spaces where underserved, underrepresented, and under-resourced young women can build confidence, strengthen their voices, access opportunities, and develop the tools to lead.

01 · BELONGING

We Create Space

We build a supportive sisterhood through mentorship, meaningful relationships, wellness, and a community where young women are seen and heard.

02 · OPPORTUNITY

We Expand Access

We connect young women to learning, career exploration, leadership training, resources, and experiences beyond their everyday environments.

03 · LEADERSHIP

We Equip Changemakers

We help mentees strengthen their decision-making, communication, character, and capacity to influence and lead change.

Our approach

Access is the beginning. Leadership is the outcome we work toward.

Our pathway connects consistent support to meaningful learning and community impact. Each stage is designed to help young women grow from discovering where they belong to using their voice and skills to lead.

Our work

Four ways we show up for young women.

Signature program

Project Bridging Mentorship

Ongoing support, sisterhood, identity development, positive relationships, and guidance for young women in grades 5–12.

Education & development

Bee University

Learning opportunities in leadership, financial literacy, STEM, critical thinking, wellness, life skills, and college and career readiness.

Health & wellbeing

Thynk Pynk

Resources & dignity

Project Pocketbook + Supply the Hive

Initiatives addressing menstrual equity and access to hygiene supplies and everyday essentials, with dignity at the center.

Why experiences matter

Meet the founder

Experience in education. A commitment to opportunity.

Patricia A. Barbee is an educator, youth specialist, advocate, and Founder and Executive Director of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs, home of Project Bridging. Her more than 25 years working with young people inform an approach that combines high expectations with relationships, access, and meaningful experiences.

“I believe in opportunity and access. Young women deserve more than encouragement. They deserve experiences, resources, relationships, and room to lead.”

Patricia A. Barbee · Founder & Executive Director
