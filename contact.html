Questions about Project Bridging

Get to know the Hive.

Who does Project Bridging serve?

Our primary programming focuses on young women in grades 5–12, with a Junior Project Bridging pathway for elementary students in grades K–4. We prioritize expanding opportunities for young women who have been underserved, underrepresented, or under-resourced.

What does Project Bridging provide?

Mentorship, youth leadership development, Bee University learning, wellness education, community engagement, experiential learning, and access to resources and relationships.

Is Project Bridging only a mentorship program?

No. Mentorship is an important foundation, but we combine it with educational enrichment, leadership, wellness, service, career exploration, and experiences beyond the classroom.

How can I support or participate?

Families can explore participation; individuals can mentor or volunteer; businesses and organizations can sponsor programs and experiences; and supporters can contribute resources or financial gifts. Use the connection section below for the current website contact pathway.
