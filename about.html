Who We Are

Established in 2022, Project Bridging is the signature youth development initiative of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs (BARYP), serving young women in Des Moines and surrounding communities.

Project Bridging is a community-centered youth development program that brings together young women, mentors, families, educators, and community partners to create meaningful opportunities for growth, connection, and exploration.

We recognize that young women experience important physical, emotional, social, and intellectual changes throughout childhood and adolescence. During these critical stages of development, consistent guidance, supportive relationships, access to resources, and meaningful experiences can make a lasting difference.

Our work extends beyond traditional mentorship by connecting education, leadership, wellness, advocacy, cultural enrichment, community engagement, and experiential learning.

Through these connections, we create spaces where young women can discover who they are, build relationships, explore their interests, develop new skills, and experience opportunities beyond their everyday environments.

We believe young women deserve more than encouragement. They deserve access to opportunities that allow them to explore what’s possible.

Our Mission
Project Bridging empowers underserved, underrepresented, and under-resourced young women by expanding equitable access to opportunities, resources, relationships, and transformative experiences.
Our Vision
We envision communities where every young woman has equitable access to the relationships, resources, education, and experiences needed to thrive, pursue her aspirations, exercise leadership, and contribute to meaningful change.
Our Purpose

Why Project Bridging Exists

Project Bridging was established in response to the gaps and barriers that can limit young women’s access to meaningful developmental opportunities.

We recognize that not every young woman has the same access to mentorship, educational enrichment, supportive networks, cultural experiences, leadership opportunities, or resources.

For some, these barriers are financial. For others, they involve transportation, limited exposure, social inequities, family circumstances, or a lack of connections to people and places that can open doors.

We also understand that young women are navigating these circumstances while developing their identities, relationships, confidence, independence, and understanding of the world.

Project Bridging exists to help close the distance between where young women are and the opportunities available to them.

Our purpose is not to define who young women should become. It is to provide the guidance, relationships, resources, and experiences that allow them to explore their possibilities and make informed choices about their lives and futures.

What We Believe

Opportunity and Access Matter.

We believe a young woman’s circumstances should not determine the limits of her opportunities.

We believe that relationships matter, representation matters, experiences matter, and young women deserve environments where their voices are valued.

We believe that growth requires more than information. It requires opportunities to participate, apply knowledge, explore different environments, build connections, and gain meaningful experiences.

We believe that young women should not only be included in conversations about their lives and communities. They should have opportunities to shape decisions and lead change.

And we believe that investing in young women is an investment in the strength and future of our communities.

The Project Bridging Community

Welcome to the Hive

At Project Bridging, we call our community The Hive.

The Hive represents connection, sisterhood, shared responsibility, collective growth, and belonging.

It is a place where young women can develop meaningful relationships, learn from one another, access supportive adults, and participate in experiences that strengthen their sense of identity and community.

Our community includes:

Mentees

Young women discovering their voices, developing skills, exploring interests, and practicing leadership.

Mentors

Trusted adults offering guidance, encouragement, perspective, and consistent support.

Families

Parents and caregivers who contribute to a young woman’s growth, stability, and participation.

Volunteers

Individuals sharing their time, knowledge, skills, and experiences.

Community Partners

Organizations, businesses, educators, and professionals who help expand access to resources and opportunities.

Supporters and Sponsors

Individuals and organizations investing in meaningful youth development experiences.

Together, we create a network of relationships and opportunities that extends beyond any single program or activity.

The Hive is where connection becomes community and opportunity becomes experience.

Our Core Values

The Principles That Guide Our Work

Empowerment

We support young women in developing the confidence, knowledge, skills, and agency to make informed decisions and advocate for themselves.

Equity

We recognize that young women face different circumstances and barriers. We work to expand access and reduce obstacles to participation.

Leadership

Community & Service

Our Foundation

Character. Leadership. Service.

Three foundational principles help shape Project Bridging’s identity and expectations.

Α — Alpha | Character

Character is reflected in our values, decisions, relationships, actions, and responsibilities.

We encourage young women to develop integrity, self-respect, accountability, and an understanding of how their choices affect themselves and others.

Λ — Lambda | Leadership

Leadership begins with self-awareness and grows through responsibility, communication, collaboration, and action.

We believe young women should have meaningful opportunities to practice leadership rather than simply learn about it.

Σ — Sigma | Service

Service connects individual growth to collective responsibility.

We encourage young women to recognize community needs, contribute their skills and perspectives, and participate in activities that benefit others.

Α + Λ + Σ = IMPACT

Together, character, leadership, and service represent our commitment to developing young women who are prepared to contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Our Approach

Developing the Whole Young Woman

A young woman’s growth is influenced by the relationships, environments, resources, opportunities, and experiences surrounding her.

For this reason, Project Bridging takes an intentional, whole-person approach to youth development.

We recognize the importance of supporting young women during critical stages of growth while addressing the barriers that may affect their ability to participate, learn, and pursue opportunities.

Our work is guided by a developmental pathway:

Access → Belonging → Empowerment → Leadership → Community Impact

This pathway reflects our belief that when young women gain access to meaningful opportunities, develop supportive relationships, strengthen their knowledge and confidence, and practice leadership, they are better positioned to contribute to their communities and shape their futures.

Our approach is grounded in seven connected areas of support and opportunity that consider the whole young woman, not simply one part of her life.

Our Journey

Established in 2022. Continuing to Beecome.

Project Bridging began with a commitment to create intentional opportunities for young women to receive guidance, develop meaningful relationships, experience new environments, and access resources that support their growth.

Since its establishment, the vision has expanded beyond mentorship to include educational enrichment, leadership development, wellness initiatives, community engagement, and experiential learning.

As Project Bridging continues to develop, our commitment remains centered on access, opportunity, equity, and meaningful experiences.

Our journey is one of continued learning, relationship-building, community investment, and growth.

We are building an organization that responds to the evolving needs of young women while creating pathways for future opportunity.

Our Founder

Meet Patricia A. Barbee

Founder & Executive Director

Patricia A. Barbee is an educator, youth specialist, and advocate with more than 25 years of experience working with young people across multiple areas of education and youth development.

Her work is rooted in a commitment to ensuring that young people have access to supportive relationships, meaningful learning opportunities, and experiences that contribute to their growth and development.

As Founder and Executive Director, she brings her experience in education, youth advocacy, mentoring, and community engagement to the continued development of Project Bridging.

Her leadership reflects a foundational belief: opportunity and access matter, and young women deserve the chance to experience the possibilities available to them.

Through Project Bridging, she works to connect young women with relationships, resources, and experiences that support their development while strengthening community involvement in youth advancement.

Part of Something Greater

Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs

Project Bridging is the signature initiative of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs (BARYP), an organization focused on expanding opportunities for underserved youth through mentorship, leadership development, educational enrichment, community connections, and experiential learning.

With the guiding motto From Risk to Resource, BARYP represents a broader commitment to bridging barriers and connecting young people with opportunities that support their advancement.

Project Bridging brings this commitment to life through focused programming for young women.

Our Commitment to Community

Meaningful Change Requires Collective Investment.

Project Bridging recognizes that supporting young women is a shared community responsibility.

No single organization can address every challenge or provide every opportunity. Strong relationships with families, educators, businesses, community organizations, and individual supporters are essential to expanding access and creating sustainable experiences.

We value partnerships that contribute knowledge, resources, opportunities, mentorship, and meaningful engagement.

Together, we can build stronger networks of support and create more opportunities for young women to learn, explore, grow, and lead.
