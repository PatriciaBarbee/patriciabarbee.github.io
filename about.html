About Project Bridging

Bridging Gaps. Expanding Opportunities.

Established in 2022, Project Bridging is the signature youth development initiative of Bridging Youth Advancement & Resource Programs (BARYP), serving young women in grades 5–12 throughout Des Moines and surrounding communities.

Project Bridging is more than a mentorship program. It is a community of young women, mentors, families, and partners brought together through shared experiences, meaningful relationships, and opportunities for personal growth.

Our programming brings together education, leadership, wellness, cultural enrichment, community service, and real-world experiences. From weekly mentorship and skill-building activities to educational excursions and community initiatives, Project Bridging provides opportunities for young women to learn, connect, explore, and grow.

Known as The Hive, our community creates a sense of sisterhood, belonging, and connection throughout each young woman’s journey of Beecoming.

Our Values

Four essential commitments

👑 Empowerment

We strengthen self-worth and leadership identity in every young woman we serve.

🌉 Access

We bridge the gaps between talent and opportunity — because talent is everywhere, but access and opportunity are not.

🌍 Opportunity

We create exposure, experiences, and relationships that expand what a young woman believes is possible.

⚖️ Social Justice

We challenge the barriers that limit equitable access, and we equip young women to advocate for themselves and their communities.

Meet the Founder

Patricia A. Barbee is an educator, youth specialist, and advocate with more than 25 years of experience working with young people. As Founder and Executive Director, she brings her experience in education and youth development to Project Bridging's commitment to opportunity, access, meaningful relationships, and experiences that expand possibilities for young women.

Who we are

Welcome to the Hive.

Project Bridging is a youth development and mentorship initiative serving young women during critical stages of growth, discovery, and transition.

We work to bridge gaps and address challenges young women may face by creating space to build confidence, knowledge, skills, healthy relationships, and a strong sense of self.

Through culturally affirming mentorship, leadership development, educational enrichment, wellness, experiential learning, and meaningful community engagement, we help young women access what they need to thrive.

We believe in opportunity and access. Young women should not be limited by their circumstances, ZIP code, or the resources available to them.
OUR MISSION

Project Bridging empowers underserved, underrepresented, and under-resourced young women by expanding equitable access to opportunities, resources, relationships, and transformative experiences.
