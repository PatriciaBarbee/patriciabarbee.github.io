🏰 The Hive Takes Disney

August 12–17, 2027. Twenty-five Project Bridging girls fly to Walt Disney World for six days: Disney STEM, teamwork, and leadership classes with full park days after each one, Magic Kingdom, and a day standing in the Atlantic Ocean — most of them for the first time.

The trip costs $2,500 a girl — and our girls are earning their seats. Each girl raises $2,000 toward her place through Project Bridging fundraisers, and Project Bridging contributes $500 for every girl. Flights, park days, classes, food, her full kit — earned, not handed over.

Help a girl reach her $2,000:

  • $50 — one girl's food for one day
  • $150 — her three Disney classes
  • $250 — her food for the whole trip
  • $500 — match Project Bridging's contribution for one girl
  • $2,000 — one girl's full raised share: her seat, earned
  • $2,500 — her complete seat

🔬 Bee the Future: Disney STEM Expedition

The sponsor-facing door of the same expedition. The six-day experience is fully costed at ≈ $53,000 — workshops, flights, lodging, meals, and a complete equipment kit for every participant — prepared through Bee University and closed out with a student STEM Innovation Showcase and the program's first formal impact report. Corporate partnerships from $2,500 to $10,000+; in-kind partnerships (footwear, personal-care kits, dental kits, cameras, travel supplies, meals) equip every girl directly.

Partnership conversations: Patricia A. Barbee · patricia.barbee@projectbridging.org
